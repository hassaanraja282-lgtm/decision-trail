# Decision Trail – Privacy Policy

Decision Trail is an Atlassian Forge app. It runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian") and makes no calls to external services.

**What it stores.** For each decision: the decision text, the Jira issue key, the Atlassian account ID of the person who logged it, and a timestamp. This is kept in Forge Key-Value Storage inside your Atlassian site's app storage.

**What it does not do.** No data leaves Atlassian. No analytics, tracking, advertising, or sharing with third parties.

**Personal data.** The only personal data is the Atlassian account ID. Decisions are deleted when their Jira issue is deleted or when the app is uninstalled.
